Fairway Next privacy

Fairway Next keeps as little about you as it can.

On your device

Your settings and your game progress are stored in your browser's local storage. Nothing else.

If you make an account

An account is optional. If you make one, we store a username you choose, your password (kept by our database host as a one-way hash that nobody can read), and your game data (progress, gear, scores and any holes you publish), so you can play on other devices. There is no email address on an account, so a forgotten password cannot be reset: the game can download a backup file of your progress instead.

Sign-up shows a picture of 5 digits to keep robots out. Your answer is checked once and not kept.

Leaderboards

Leaderboards show your username and your scores to other players. They are casual and not cheat-proof.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling or sharing of data. There are no purchases.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings, then Delete my data. It removes your account, your cloud data, and everything on this device. It cannot be undone.

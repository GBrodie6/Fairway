Fairway Next privacy

Fairway Next keeps as little about you as it can.

On your device

Your settings and your game progress are stored in your browser's local storage. Nothing else.

If you make an account

An account is optional. If you make one, we store your email address, a display name you choose, and your game data (progress, gear, and scores) with our database host, so you can play on other devices.

Leaderboards

Leaderboards show your display name and your scores to other players. They are casual and not cheat-proof.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling or sharing of data. There are no purchases.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings, then Delete my data. It removes your account, your cloud data, and everything on this device. It cannot be undone.
